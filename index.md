# TimeSmear Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 November 2026

TimeSmear is a camera app made by **Boot & Barrel Studio** (Robert Ambidge, United Kingdom). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **TimeSmear does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your phone.**

## What TimeSmear does not do

- It does not collect any personal information.
- It has no accounts, sign-ins or registration.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- It does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
- It does not sell or share any data with anyone.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Camera:** to show the camera preview and capture the image lines that make up your photos. Camera images are processed only on your phone and are never sent anywhere.
- **Vibration:** to buzz when a shot finishes, if you turn that setting on.
- **Storage (Android 8 and 9 only):** to save your photos to your phone's Pictures folder. Newer versions of Android don't need this permission.

TimeSmear never asks to see your photo library. It can only see the pictures it made itself.

## Your photos and settings

- Photos you take are saved as image files on your phone, in **Pictures/TimeSmear**. They stay there until you delete them, either in TimeSmear's gallery or in your usual gallery app.
- Your app settings (such as mode, slit direction and slice width) are stored on your phone so they're remembered next time.
- If you uninstall TimeSmear, its settings are removed. Photos already saved in your Pictures folder stay there unless you delete them.
- TimeSmear doesn't use Android's automatic app backup, so its settings aren't copied to any backup. Your photos are ordinary image files in your Pictures folder, and you control them like any other photo.

## Sharing your photos

If you choose to share a photo from TimeSmear, your phone's share menu sends it to the app you pick (for example, a messaging app or email). From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies. TimeSmear doesn't see or keep any record of what you share or who you share it with.

## Children

TimeSmear doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever started collecting data, this policy would be updated, and the app's Google Play listing would be updated, before that happened.
